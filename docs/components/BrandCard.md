# BrandCard
The tilted card stack: an `orange-fill` front card over a `panel` back card. The signature image of the brand.

Provide: a kicker for the top (`CURRENTLY BREWING`, `THE PROMPT`, `TODAY'S CONSEQUENCE`) and a short Fraunces line for the bottom. For game content, wrap the answer in `<em>` to italicise and underline it.

- Front card tilts +5°, back card −8°. Never more than two cards in the stack on the web; media may fan three.
- Use `radius-lg` and `elev-card` on the web; in 1200px media use `radius-card` and `elev-media`.
- Real game content only. Don't write fake cards.
