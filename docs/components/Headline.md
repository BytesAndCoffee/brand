# Headline
The hero pattern: a mono kicker, a Fraunces headline with one italic orange accent, and a Manrope lead.

Provide: a kicker (a short category line), a headline that ends with a period, the accent word, and an optional one-sentence lead.

- Exactly one `bc-accent` per headline, and it's the funny or load-bearing word ("terrible", "Regrets", "live").
- Kicker is always uppercase `orange-dark`; the lead is `text-body`, 34 characters wide at most.
- Left-aligned. Never centre a hero headline.
