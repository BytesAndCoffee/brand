# Wordmark
The B tile plus a two-line name: the mark for Bytes & Coffee and each sub-brand.

Provide: the two lines of the name. The first line is uppercase in `ink`; the second, in `orange`, is the word people remember (COFFEE, DECISIONS, REGRETS).

- The tile is always `ink` with an `on-ink` Fraunces ExtraBold **B**, corner `radius-tile`. Scale tile and text together; the tile is 56px beside a 26px second line.
- Put it top-left on pages and social images. Keep one tile-height of clear space around it.
- Don't recolour the tile orange, outline it, or set the name in Fraunces.
